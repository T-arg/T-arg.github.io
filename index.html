Welcome to TEAM a.r.g.

TEAM a.r.g. (Arduino Retro Gaming) is a creative collective dedicated to bringing classic gaming experiences to modern hardware.

Started by Joeri Gantois (JO3RI) when the Arduboy arrived, we've been crafting games, tools, and experiences that blend retro charm with modern technology.

What We Do

We create games and tools for Arduino-based platforms, focusing on:

  • Retro-inspired game development
  • Arduboy hardware projects
  • Open-source tools and utilities
  • Community collaboration

Connect with us on GitHub to see our latest projects and contributions.

About TEAM a.r.g.

Our Story

TEAM a.r.g. was founded by Joeri Gantois (JO3RI) with a passion for retro gaming and creative coding. What started as a personal project has evolved into a community of like-minded developers and enthusiasts.

Our Mission

We believe in the power of retro gaming aesthetics combined with modern technology. Our mission is to create engaging, fun experiences while fostering an open-source community where developers can collaborate and learn.

Technology

We primarily work with:

  • Arduino & Arduboy: The heart of our hardware projects
  • Web Technologies: For tools and online experiences
  • Open Source: All our projects are freely available on GitHub
